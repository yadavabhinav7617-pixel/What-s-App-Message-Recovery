Privacy Policy & Data Security Disclosure

Privacy First Guarantee

1. Overview & Commitment

NotifyVault ("we", "our", or "the app") is designed as a privacy-focused local notification vault for Android. Your privacy is our highest priority.

NotifyVault operates 100% locally on your device. All captured notifications, message texts, timestamps, and metadata are saved strictly inside your device's local database and are never uploaded, transmitted, or shared with any external server, cloud service, or third party.

2. Android Notification Access Permission

To provide its core functionality (saving a local copy of incoming notifications from supported messaging apps such as WhatsApp), NotifyVault requests Android's BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE permission.

  • Explicit Authorization: Notification Access is disabled by default and requires your explicit consent through Android system settings.
  • Scope of Access: The app processes incoming notification content exposed by the Android system while Notification Access is enabled.
  • No Database Tampering: NotifyVault does not access private WhatsApp files, private chat databases, or any unauthorized app storage. It processes only what Android displays in your notification tray.

3. Information Collected & Storage

The app stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Notification sender name and conversation title
  • Notification text message content
  • Timestamp of when the notification was posted and captured
  • Source app package name (e.g., com.whatsapp)

This data remains stored in a local SQLite/Room database on your device and is accessible only to you through the app interface.

4. Third-Party Sharing & Cloud Services

NotifyVault contains:

  • NO Analytics SDKs (No Google Analytics, Firebase, Mixpanel, etc.)
  • NO Advertising SDKs (No AdMob, Unity Ads, etc.)
  • NO External Server Connections (No data transmitted over the internet)
  • NO Cloud Backups

5. User Control & Data Retention

You maintain full control over your saved notification history:

  • Manual Deletion: You can delete individual messages or clear all saved records at any time from the app interface.
  • Automatic Cleanup: Built-in retention options allow automatically deleting entries older than 7, 30, or 90 days.
  • Data Export: You can export a JSON copy of your saved data locally via the Storage settings using Android's Storage Access Framework.
  • Revoking Access: You can disable Notification Access at any time via Android System Settings > Notification Access.

6. Children's Privacy

NotifyVault does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children. Because all data remains stored locally on the user's device, no personal data is collected by us from any user.
